“LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch”.

      Gone are the days of heavy textbooks and one-size-fits-all learning. Today, students can explore ebooks, receive instant AI feedback, and access a world of knowledge with just a click. Artificial intelligence has become increasingly common in today's society, not just in education but it has made it's way to being the tool for the simplest of task.

      The statement "LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot must be used as a tool and not a crutch." Is important as there is a significant difference between using AI to improve learning and to use AI to learn for me.
As a student, this statement speaks to still being repsonsible for my own learning, critical thinking and academic work instead of being over dependent on an AI system.

      A crutch provides support when someone needs assistance but using it unecessarily can prevent that person from developing independence.
      I typically use LLMs before studying to break down materials, during studying to give different multiple choice or short answer questions in order to test my knowledge. I simply take this feedback in order to sharpen my skills to prepare me to do any test, assignment or to break down lectures that might seem challenging.

      Using AI to complete an entire assignement without actually having a full understanding would make it a crutch. Instead, I generally attempt the work myself and use the AI for guidnace wherever I fall short. It is recommended by UNSECO(2024) that students develop the ability to use AI responsibly, ethically and critically.

Therefore, LLMs can improve my education whenever it is used correctly and responsiblily, not just to complete a whole assignment but to break down parts I may find difficult. My entire objective as a student is to be a better thinker and a better learner, with AI serving as an assistant rather than a replacement of my own critical thinking and efforts.
